Survey on Personality and Immediate Impressions of Images

This study looks at whether the words people first think of when seeing an image relate to their personality.

  • You will answer your age and gender, take a personality test (MPI), and type 3 words for each of 9 images. This takes about 10 minutes.
  • You must be 18 or older. Taking part is voluntary and unpaid.
  • Every question must be answered to continue. You can quit at any time by closing this page, and nothing will be saved.
  • No names or contact details are collected. Because responses are anonymous, they cannot be withdrawn once you submit.
  • Anonymous results will be used for research and publications.

JP

これから、いくつかの画像が1枚ずつ表示されます。各画像について、次のことを行ってください：

  • 画像を見て、最初に思い浮かんだ印象を3つほど書いてください。
  • 各画像について20秒以内に答えてください。
  • 思いついた順番で入力してください。深く考えすぎないでください。

正解・不正解はありません。これはあなたの第一印象や直感的な反応を知るためのものです。

EN

You will be shown a series of images, one at a time. For each image, please do the following:

  • Please write three words that immediately come to mind when you see the image.
  • You will have 20 seconds to respond to each image.
  • Enter your thoughts in the order they occur — do not overthink your responses.

There are no wrong answers. This is about your first impressions and instinctive reactions.
